# IREM entry offer contract — 2026-07-13

The IREM entry hero no longer repeats the price. The purchase price remains visible in the self-study offer card and is the single pricing surface used by the checkout flow.

The landing smoke contract therefore requires the offer price, hero CTA, final checkout CTA, sticky CTA, and IREM section stack; it does not require `.hero-price`.

This is a route-local landing composition change. It does not change the IREM route boundary, payment endpoint, or shared token contract.
