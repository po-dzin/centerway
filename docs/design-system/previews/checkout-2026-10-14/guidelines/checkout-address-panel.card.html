Address panel

The right half of /pay/checkout (AddressPanel.tsx). The offer panel's raised material, because it holds the page's one primary action. It asks a name, an address (signed in: names the account and offers «Інша адреса») and one messenger, Telegram or WhatsApp, chosen by two radios on the secondary plate. Fine print sits at the foot.
